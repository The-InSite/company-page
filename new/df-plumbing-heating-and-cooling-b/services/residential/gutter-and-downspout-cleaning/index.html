Why Gutters and Downspouts Are Vital for Your Home

In the Pacific Northwest, rain isn’t a season — it’s a constant. That sustained rainfall means a constant risk of water damage to your home. A properly functioning gutter and downspout system channels water off your roof and directs it away from your foundation. Without it, stormwater pools around your home, erodes soil, damages landscaping, and can seep into basements and crawl spaces.
