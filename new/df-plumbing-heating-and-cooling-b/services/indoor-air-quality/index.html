Why Indoor Air Quality Matters in Portland Homes

Indoor air quality (IAQ) is more than just comfort — it’s a key factor in your health, HVAC efficiency, and even your home’s structural integrity. According to the EPA, indoor air can be two to five times more polluted than outdoor air. In Portland, this is often due to damp weather, seasonal pollen, wildfire smoke, and mold growth in older homes. Pollutants such as pet dander, volatile organic compounds (VOCs), dust mites, and bacteria circulate through HVAC systems and ductwork, spreading allergens throughout your home.
