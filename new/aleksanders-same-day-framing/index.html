About Aleksander Gallery

Family-owned and operated since 1974, Aleksander Gallery brings over 30 years of custom framing expertise to every project. From our fully equipped Austin workshop, we offer traditional and modern frame-making techniques, museum-quality framing, object framing, canvas stretching, tapestry framing, and restoration services. We design and produce unique presentation pieces for corporate, sports, and military facilities, and we can find or match virtually any existing frame.

Our Framing Services

Custom framing solutions for every need — art, memorabilia, corporate displays, and more.

From single artwork to large institutional installations, we handle every framing project with precision and care.

SERVICE 01
Custom Frame Design

We create custom frames to protect and enhance art objects for display, working with customers to design the exact frame they desire. Design service costs are included in the frame price.

SERVICE 02
Museum Quality Framing

Conservation and museum mounting using cotton rag matboards, UV-filtering glass, and museum glass to preserve and protect valuable artwork for generations.

SERVICE 03
Shadow Box Framing

Custom shadow boxes for three-dimensional objects, memorabilia, diplomas, certificates, military items, and sports collectibles.

How It Works

Discuss your framing needs with our experts. We help you select from thousands of frame styles, mat options, and glass types to achieve the perfect look.
