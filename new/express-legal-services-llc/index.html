Who We Are & What We Do

Express Legal Services LLC is a Atlanta-based process serving company founded by Rod McClellan after 20 years in the relationship management and legal services industries. We are dedicated to going above and beyond to exceed your expectations with every order. We hand-pick only the finest process servers so you can be assured your documents and all interactions are handled professionally. We are members of the National Association of Professional Process Servers (NAPPS) and the Georgia Association of Professional Process Servers (GAPPS), and hold court appointments in Fulton, Forsyth, Gwinnett, Cobb, DeKalb, and Clayton counties.

Our Process

How It Works

01

Submit Your Order

Send your documents via email, mail, fax, or arrange a pickup if you're in the Atlanta area. We accept orders from attorneys, law firms, and companies nationwide.

02

First Attempt Within 1–3 Days

We make the first service attempt within 1 to 3 business days for routine service, or the same day for rush orders. We schedule attempts strategically — typically after 5:30 PM on weekdays and on Saturdays — to maximize success.

03

Real-Time Status Updates

After every attempt, you receive a timely update. If we spot any issues with service, we contact you immediately so we can make decisions together and avoid unnecessary costs.
