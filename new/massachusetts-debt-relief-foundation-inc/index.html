Our Story

Created in June 2011, MDRF is a 501(c)(3) non-profit law firm offering free comprehensive legal advice on all debt matters so Massachusetts families may achieve their financial goals, regardless of their ability to afford a lawyer. We provide pro bono legal representation in chapter 7 bankruptcy and other debt matters when appropriate. Program Attorney Maegan Hurley and Founding Director Attorney Richard Ravosa have been recognized for their innovative free debt relief legal services. MDRF relies on volunteer attorneys and public donations to help low-income families move beyond debt.

To improve the quality of life for those unduly burdened with excessive debt by providing compassionate and effective pro bono legal representation in order to eliminate debt and restore peace of mind.
