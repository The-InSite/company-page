About MARI D MUA MAKEUP STUDIO

Mariana is a luxury bridal makeup artist, beauty educator, and founder of Mari DMUA Beauty Studio in Miami, FL. With a background in business and sales, Mariana combined her entrepreneurial spirit with her passion for artistry to create a premier bridal beauty destination. She believes makeup is more than just a service—it’s an empowering experience that enhances natural beauty and elevates confidence.
