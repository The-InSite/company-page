Our Refinishing Services

Professional Refinishing for Every Surface

From bathtubs and showers to tile walls and countertops, we restore your bathroom surfaces to like-new condition — fast, affordable, and built to last.

01

Bathtub Refinishing

Restore your worn or damaged bathtub to like-new condition in just one day. We refinish all types of bathtubs using proprietary submersion coatings for a perfect, permanent finish.

02

Shower Resurfacing

Revitalize your shower surfaces without the cost and hassle of full replacement. Our expert technicians restore your shower to a fresh, clean appearance.

03

Ceramic Tile Refinishing

Bring new life to old or discolored ceramic tile walls and floors. Our refinishing process saves you time and money compared to full tile replacement.
