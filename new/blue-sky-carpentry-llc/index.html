Blue Sky Carpentry LLC was founded in 1999 by master carpenter Blue Alan Way. With over three decades of hands-on experience, Blue and his team have served residential and commercial clients across Michigan and Texas, including the Zen Buddhist Temple of Ann Arbor, Ypsilanti Standard Printing Company, and the Ypsilanti Natural Food Co-op. Now based in Austin, TX, Blue Sky Carpentry offers custom furniture design, architectural framing, outdoor structures, and complete renovation services. The studio is guided by a Japanese-influenced design philosophy balanced with modern elegance, and a strong commitment to using recycled and locally sourced wood whenever possible.
