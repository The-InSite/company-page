How Do Heat Pumps Work?

Heat pumps don’t generate heat; they move it. In winter, they pull heat from the outdoor air and transfer it inside your home. In summer, the process reverses, removing heat from indoors and releasing it outside. This is done using a refrigerant that cycles between an outdoor and an indoor unit. Because they transfer heat instead of creating it, heat pumps are highly energy-efficient and provide both heating and cooling in one system. To top

Heat Pumps vs. Furnaces in Portland: What’s Best?

For Portland’s mild climate, heat pumps are often the better choice because they provide both heating and cooling with excellent energy efficiency. They can lower utility costs and eliminate the need for a separate AC system. Furnaces, on the other hand, are ideal for very cold climates where stronger heating output is needed. Many Portland homeowners choose heat pumps for year-round comfort and long-term savings.
