What Is Trenchless Sewer Repair?

Trenchless sewer repair—also known as no-dig sewer line replacement—uses advanced technology to restore underground pipes with minimal surface disruption. Instead of digging long trenches, our experts create small access points and use equipment to either reline the existing pipe or burst the old pipe and replace it with a new one. These solutions are designed to handle common problems like root intrusion, cracked clay pipes, and corroded cast iron lines. The result? How Trenchless Sewer Line Replacement Works

Pipe Lining (Cured-in-Place Pipe) Pipe lining involves inserting a flexible, resin-coated liner into the damaged pipe. Once cured, it forms a smooth, durable interior wall that restores flow and prevents leaks. This method is excellent for addressing cracks, leaks, and smaller root intrusions. Pipe Bursting When pipes are too damaged for lining, sewer pipe bursting is the answer. A bursting head breaks apart the old pipe while simultaneously pulling a new, high-strength pipe into place. Signs You Need Sewer Line Repair

Not every plumbing issue means your sewer line is failing, but certain warning signs should never be ignored: - Frequent clogs or backups in multiple drains - Gurgling noises coming from toilets or sinks - Persistent sewer odors inside or outside the home - Unexplained patches of lush, green grass in your yard - Foundation cracks, sinkholes, or soggy ground

Trenchless Sewer Repair: Homeowners Questions

What is the difference between pipe lining and pipe bursting?Pipe lining creates a new interior within the old pipe, while pipe bursting breaks apart the existing pipe and replaces it with a new one. How long does trenchless sewer repair last?On average, trenchless pipe solutions last 40–50 years or more when installed by licensed professionals.
