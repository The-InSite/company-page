Signs You Need Professional Outdoor Drain Cleaning

Outdoor drainage problems don’t always happen overnight. Watch for these warning signs: - Water pooling near patios, driveways, or walkways - Slow-draining exterior grates or yard drains - Gurgling noises in pipes after rainstorms - Bad odors near outdoor drains or basements - Frequent basement flooding or damp crawl spaces If you’ve noticed any of these, call a drain plumber before the issue escalates.

Tips to Prevent Outdoor Drain Clogs

Homeowners can reduce the risk of clogs by following a few simple tips: - Install grate covers to stop leaves and debris from entering drains. - Schedule French drain cleaning or inspections before the rainy season. - Avoid planting trees with aggressive root systems near your outdoor drains. - Flush drains with clean water occasionally to keep water moving.
