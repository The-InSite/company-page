What areas do you service?

We service Atlanta and the entire Metro Atlanta area. For large events we travel all over the Southeast and nationwide.

How do I place an order?

Returning and new customers can use the Get a Quote or Book now option on our site, or CALL NOW or EMAIL now

How soon do I need to place my order?

We are often able to offer same or next day service, during busy times most orders can be confirmed 72 hours in advance

Do you charge a delivery/ Setup Fee?

Yes, this fee varies based on event location and start time

How long do balloons last?

Air filled and organic balloons often last a long time indoors. Helium balloons last 8-10 hours, but can be treated to last longer for an additional fee. If you are having a multi--day event, be sure to let us know

Do you remove the balloons after my event?

Yes, for an additional fee.

Can I meet with someone to help me decide?

Absolutely! We regularly do consultations by appointment.
