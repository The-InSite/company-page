About

NYC's Go-To Plumbing Experts

A&E NYC Plumbing is a family-owned and operated business with decades of experience serving New York City homeowners and businesses. Our fully licensed and insured team of plumbers and certified technicians is prepared to handle any plumbing, heating, or water damage restoration challenge. We require our plumbers to pursue continuing education so they stay ahead of the latest industry changes. From simple faucet repairs to complex gas line installations, we approach every job with the same commitment to quality, safety, and customer satisfaction.

Our Plumbing, Heating & Water Damage Services

Everything Your Home or Business Needs — Done Right the First Time

From emergency repairs to full installations, we offer comprehensive plumbing services across Manhattan and New York City.

SERVICE 01
Dishwasher Repair

Our licensed plumbers and certified technicians diagnose and repair all dishwasher issues, from unusual noises and improper draining to units that won't power on. We handle both simple and complex configurations.

SERVICE 02
Drain Repair

From minor drain cleaning and resealing to resolving collapsed pipes, tree root intrusion, and corrosion, our team quickly identifies and fixes all drain issues to prevent further damage.

SERVICE 03
Faucet & Sink Repair and Installation

A malfunctioning faucet can spike your water bills. Our experienced technicians provide fast, accurate diagnosis and repair of faucet and sink problems — and handle new installations with precision.
