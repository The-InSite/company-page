We're Ben and Colleen

We're Ben and Colleen, wedding photographers based in Atlanta, Georgia. We met in photography school in 2008 and have been photographing weddings together ever since. Our philosophy on wedding photography is to treat every moment as if it were the single most important thing to ever happen. Our cameras are constantly at our faces all day, capturing all the candid moments and real emotion. We hate the idea of putting arbitrary time limits on wedding coverage — we're there to tell your story from beginning to end.
