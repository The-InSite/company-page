Our Story

About Us

We're Ben and Colleen, wedding photographers based in Atlanta, Georgia. We met in photography school in 2008 and have been photographing weddings together ever since.

"Our philosophy on wedding photography is to treat every moment as if it were the single most important thing to ever happen."

Our cameras are constantly at our faces all day, capturing all the candid moments and real emotion. We hate the idea of putting arbitrary time limits on wedding coverage — we're there to tell your story from beginning to end.

We love everything about weddings, and we're super excited that you're considering making us a part of your wedding day!

What We Offer

Our Services

Wedding Photography

Full-day coverage capturing every candid moment and real emotion from beginning to end. Two photographers working together to tell your complete love story.

Engagement Sessions

Intimate sessions at meaningful locations — from Inman Park neighborhoods to Sweetwater Creek sunsets. Let us help you feel natural in front of the camera.

Elopements

Whether it's Jackson Hole or your own backyard, we travel wherever love takes us. Intimate celebrations documented with the same dedication as grand affairs.

Film + Digital

We shoot both digital and film for a rich, timeless quality. From dreamy Minolta lenses to medium format Mamiya, every image is crafted with intention.

Destination Weddings

From San Francisco to Beverly Hills, we've captured love stories across the country. Available for travel to your dream destination anywhere in the world.

Full Story Coverage

No arbitrary time limits. We're there from the first mimosa toast to the last dance, because your story deserves to be told completely and beautifully.
