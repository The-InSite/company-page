Why Is My Air Conditioner Blowing Warm Air?

An AC system blowing hot air often points to refrigerant issues, compressor problems, or thermostat settings. Sometimes it’s as simple as incorrect settings or a dirty filter, but it can also indicate a more serious mechanical issue. Why Did My Air Conditioner Stop Working?

A sudden AC failure can happen for several reasons, from electrical issues to worn-out components. Tripped breakers, failed capacitors, or overheating can all cause the system to shut down. How Long Does an AC Unit Last?

The lifespan of your AC depends on the type of system and how well it’s maintained. - Central AC systems – Typically last 12–15 years with regular maintenance - Mini-split systems – Often last 15–20 years due to efficient operation You should consider replacement if your system needs frequent repairs, struggles to cool your home, or causes rising energy bills. How Much Does the Average AC Repair Cost in Portland?

AC repair costs in Portland can vary depending on the issue, but most homeowners can expect a fairly wide range. Smaller repairs like capacitor replacements or thermostat fixes typically fall between $150 and $400, while mid-range repairs such as refrigerant recharges or fan motor replacements can range from $400 to $900. Larger repairs, like compressor issues, can exceed $1,000. We always provide clear, upfront pricing before any work begins, so you know exactly what you’re paying for.
