Clogs

Hair, dirt, mineral buildup, soap, and even small toys can block shower and bathtub drains. If you can’t remove the clog manually or with a vinegar-and-baking-soda solution, it’s time to call a professional plumber. Attempting to use supermarket plumbing snakes can worsen the situation and damage your porcelain fixtures. Chemical drain cleaners often fail to work as advertised and can exacerbate the problem. Water Pressure

Low water pressure can be due to an issue with your shower head or faucet. However, it could also indicate buildup in the pipes or a broken line allowing dirt and silt to block your plumbing. A professional plumber can determine the cause and recommend appropriate solutions. To top

Noises & Bad Odors

Thumps or rattling sounds from your plumbing indicate that something is loose. Addressing these noises promptly can prevent further damage and costly repairs. Bad smells emanating from your bathtub or shower drain suggest a problem, such as a clog or mold.
