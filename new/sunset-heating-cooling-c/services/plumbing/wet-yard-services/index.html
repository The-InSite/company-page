French Drains

French drains are one of the most effective solutions for managing excess water in your yard. These systems consist of a trench filled with gravel or rock and a perforated pipe that redirects surface water and groundwater away from your property. Here’s how French drains work: - Water Collection: The trench collects excess water from the surface and underground. - Filtration: Gravel and rock filter out debris, preventing clogs in the perforated pipe. - Water Transport: The perforated pipe, installed with a slight slope, directs water away from problem areas to a safe discharge point.
