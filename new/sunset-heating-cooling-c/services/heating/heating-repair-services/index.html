Is It Better to Repair or Replace My Heating System?

The decision to repair or replace your heater depends on its age, repair history, and overall efficiency. If your system is more than 15 years old, requires frequent repairs, or struggles to heat your home evenly, replacement may be more cost-effective long term. However, newer systems with minor issues can often be repaired successfully. A professional evaluation can help you choose the most practical and budget-friendly option.
