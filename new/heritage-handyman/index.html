How Can We Help?

Repair. Install. Create.

Whether you need something fixed, installed, or completely reimagined, Heritage Handyman has the skills and experience to get it done right.

SERVICE 01

Repair

Keeping your home in top shape takes care and attention to detail. We handle electrical fixtures, drywall, leaky faucets, fences, decks, sprinklers, doors, windows, cabinetry, and more — from minor touch-ups to major fixes inside and out.

SERVICE 02

Install

From TVs and smart home systems to antique hardware and fine art, we install everything with precision and care. We handle furniture assembly, lighting upgrades, EV chargers, kitchen and bath upgrades, outdoor kitchens, security systems, and more.

SERVICE 03

Create

We help reimagine your spaces. From full-scale outdoor lighting designs to artistic room makeovers, podcast studios, custom decks, outdoor kitchens, and creative space conversions — we bring vision and craftsmanship to every detail.

Craftsmanship You Can Count On

Heritage Handyman was founded by artist and craftsman Scott Ratner with a simple mission: to bring exceptional care and skill to every home and business in Austin. With over 18 years of experience, our team handles everything from minor repairs to full creative transformations — always with the same dedication to quality. We serve classic and modern properties throughout Central Texas, from Tarrytown to Circle C and beyond.
