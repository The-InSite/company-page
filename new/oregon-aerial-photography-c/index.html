Capabilities

Oblique Aerial Photographs

shot at an angle to encompass the horizon and surrounding area of the site. This view is very useful in showing the building site relationship to the surrounding vicinity. This birds eye view allows visual reference to surrounding properties, roadways, shopping centers, and other main areas of interest pertaining to the site. Obliques are identified two ways. 'High or Low' obliques.

Vertical Aerial Photography

We can shoot a near vertical image where the aerial views are shot over the top of the site rather from an oblique angle. We do not have a port in our plane at this time therefore the verticals are done by putting the plane in a steep bank. These overhead views are useful in designing layouts of large land development projects. These are especially useful in preliminary presentations for potential clients.

On-Site Photography

available. This offers photography at the on-site location for ground level views.

About the Team

Oregon is owned and operated by Deborah & Brian Cruikshank. We are both pilots, and we started the business to combine our two passions, the love of flying, and the love of photography. It doesn't get any better than this!! Brian has a Masters degree in electrical engineering from Virginia Tech. He currently works as a computer engineer for Intel. Deb has a Masters in Architecture from the University of Colorado at Denver. She has worked among the top architecture companies throughout the Denver Metro area. Deb has also worked in aviation over 3 years as a customer service representative, so she is dedicated to giving the best customer service possible. She has been delivering Aerial Photography to many happy customers for over 5 years now. She has won numerous awards over the years including Best in Show at the 2010 PAPA Conference for her Picture of Long's Peak . Oregon Aerial Photography is a Professional Aerial Photographers Association certified aerial photographer in Oregon.
