Our Crawler

Last updated: October 25, 2026

If you found StaticStudio in your server logs, this page explains who we are, what our crawler does on your site, and how to stop it.

Who runs it

The crawler is run by The InSite (theinsite.dev). It identifies itself on every request with this User-Agent:

StaticStudio/1.0 (+https://theinsite.dev/crawler)

Its product token, the name to use in robots.txt, is StaticStudio.

Why it visits

We rebuild existing websites as fast, static sites. To do that, the crawler copies a site's public pages and pictures so the new site can carry over the owner's own content. It visits a site when:

  • the site's owner, or a web agency working for them, asked us to rebuild it or to prepare a preview of the new version;
  • we are testing that the crawler reads a particular kind of site correctly, on a small number of sites, at the slowest pace described below.

We do not sell or share what we collect, and we do not use it to train AI models.

What it collects

  • Public pages, as any visitor's browser would receive them.
  • The pictures and documents (such as PDF menus or brochures) those pages show or link to.

It does not log in, fill in or submit forms, run your pages' scripts, or visit anything behind a password. Each file it downloads is checked, and anything that is not a plain picture or document is discarded.

How it behaves

  • It reads your robots.txt before its first request to your site, and never requests a URL that robots.txt disallows for StaticStudio (or, if no group names it, for *).
  • It honours Crawl-delay.
  • It waits at least one second between page requests to the same host. Pictures are fetched in small groups of ten, with a pause after each group.
  • It slows down when your server answers 429 or 503, and waits as long as a Retry-After header asks.
  • Each visit stops after a fixed number of pages; it does not keep crawling your site on a schedule.
  • It follows links only within the part of the site it was asked to copy. A picture on those pages may be served from another host, such as an image service; that host's robots.txt is read and honoured too.

How to block it

Add these lines to your robots.txt. The crawler reads them before its next request and stops:

User-agent: StaticStudio
Disallow: /

To keep it out of only part of your site, disallow just that path, for example Disallow: /private/.

How to opt out for good

Email crawler@theinsite.dev with your site's address. We add the domain to our opt-out list, which the crawler checks before every visit and every request. A domain on the list is never fetched again, whatever its robots.txt says, until you ask us to remove it.

Questions or a problem

If the crawler caused trouble on your site, or you have a question about it, write to us. Please include your site's address and, if you can, the time and the IP address from your logs.
