The Vision of The InSite Company

Preview Flow

What happens after
you request a preview

The request flow is designed to move fast: you show us your current site, tell us the direction, and get a short-lived preview window that makes the next decision obvious.

Step 01

Drop in your current site

We start from the site you already have. The flow captures the URL, your business name, your contact email, and the positioning you want the new version to emphasize.

Step 02

Choose the direction

Pick the flavor that matters most to you, choose a theme manually, or simply use my logo colors so the output starts closer to your existing brand.

Step 03

Get the preview by email

Once it’s ready, you get a link by email and can review the new version without touching your current site or disrupting anything live.

Step 04

Keep it live for 24 hours

The preview stays up for 24 hours, then gets a reminder, a deletion warning, and an option to extend it for a week before it disappears.

Every email in that sequence should push toward the same decision: if the new version feels right, let’s go live.
