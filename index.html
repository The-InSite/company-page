The Vision of The InSite Company

Preview Flow

What happens after
you request a preview

Three short steps: your site, the look, and where to send it. The rest we read from the site itself.

Step 01

Drop in your current site

We start from the site you already have: its address, your business name, the type of business and where it is.

Step 02

Choose the look

Keep the colours your site uses today, or pick a direction. Your content stays exactly as it is.

Step 03

Get the preview by email

Once it’s ready, you get a link by email and can review the new version without touching your current site or disrupting anything live.

Step 04

Keep it live for 24 hours

The preview stays up for 24 hours, then gets a reminder, a deletion warning, and an option to extend it for a week before it disappears.

If the new version feels right, we take it live with you.

Our Process

From first call
to live site

A streamlined, algorithm-accelerated four-step process that respects your time, keeps you in control, and delivers measurable results.

01

Discovery

A focused 30-minute conversation to understand your business, your clients, your goals, and what's holding your current presence back.

02

Site Analysis

Our algorithm performs a deep audit of your current online presence and delivers a prioritized transformation roadmap — clear, specific, and actionable.

03

Design & Build

We design, write, and build your modernized site at speed — with your approval at every milestone. Nothing goes live until you're satisfied.

04

Launch & Refine

We handle launch and handle everything after it — continuously refining performance, content, and design as your business grows.

Your current site stays live the entire time we build. Nothing goes down, and nothing gets replaced, until you've reviewed the new one and given the go-ahead.
